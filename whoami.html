FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Privacy & Telemetry FAQ

Common questions regarding browser fingerprinting, network telemetry, and our zero-trust architecture.

What makes this different from generic IP checkers?
Most IP checkers only display your public IPv4 address. Spyboy Specter Recon Lab inspects DNS resolver paths, WebRTC candidate leaks, browser APIs, installed system fonts, GPU fingerprints, storage flags, and locale offsets to show the exact high-entropy fingerprint ad-tech and tracking companies assemble.
Is my data sent to Spyboy servers?
No. Every detection runs inside your browser with open client-side APIs (ipapi, ipify, EDNS). Nothing is stored, logged, or transmitted back. You can view the page source or inspect DevTools Network tab to verify 100% client isolation.
Can I link to this scanner or embed it?
Yes—feel free to share Spyboy Specter Recon Lab or fork the repository to serve it privately within your organization. Attribution is appreciated but not required.
